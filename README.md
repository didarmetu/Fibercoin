### What is Fibercoin(FBC)?

Fibercoin – Fast Blockchain Cash is an open-source, blockchain-based cryptocurrency that aims to provide users with fast transactions, low fees, high network decentralization, and a simple, secure, private, and stable digital transaction experience.

Fibercoin was launched to address the needs of smaller participants in the cryptocurrency ecosystem and aims to be a sustainable long-term cryptocurrency project with practical uses beyond the blockchain itself.

Masternodes provide additional network functionality and participate in the Fibercoin reward system. A total of 10,000 FBC is required as collateral to operate a masternode, while the minimum stake age is 5 hours.

The Fibercoin project also aims to support projects and services that can use FBC as a payment method. Community participation can help shape the direction and practical use of the Fibercoin ecosystem.

Fibercoin uses Proof of Stake for ongoing block production after its initial Proof-of-Work phase. This design avoids the continuous competitive mining required by Proof-of-Work-focused networks, allowing Fibercoin to maintain its blockchain with substantially lower energy requirements.
